Level 1 (Smoothing 0): round-corner channels on the grid — squeeze-free by construction.
Level 2 (Smoothing > 0): the passage centerlines are smoothed into curves (rounded-corner splines through the cell centers), giving the organic / U-turn feel while staying on the same 2-axis grid. Corners stay connected at junctions (only mid-corridor bends smooth), and the cut distance is capped at ½·cell so a curve's inner radius stays > ½·passage and it never bows past a neighbor — i.e. still no squeeze points within the slider's range.
